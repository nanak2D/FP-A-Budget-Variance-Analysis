# FP&A Budget Variance Analysis
## Project Overview
This project examines 2025 financial performance across 10 cost centers using Microsoft Excel. My objective was to identify areas of overspending, assess budget performance and evaluate proposed budget reductions for 2026.
## Financial Performance
| Metric | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Approved Budget | $8,870,684.76 |
| Actual Expenditure | $9,095,144.35 |
| Net Budget Variance | -$224,459.59 |
| Budget Variance % | -2.53% |

## Key Findings

- Actual expenditure exceeded the approved annual budget by 2.53%.
- Cost Centers 9, 10 and 8 accounted for 66.94% of gross unfavorable variance across cost centers.
- Cost Center 9 recorded the largest budget overrun of $68,881.25.
- Account 1 within Cost Center 9 exceeded its budget in 10 of the 12 months. February, March and April accounted for 50.21% of its net annual overrun.
- Cost Centers 1 and 2 recorded combined favorable variances of $25,813.15.

## Scenario and Sensitivity Analysis

I evaluated different expenditure growth and budget reduction scenarios to determine the spending thresholds at which proposed budget cuts could remain feasible without creating unfavorable variances.

## Management Recommendations

I recommend prioritizing expenditure reviews in Cost Centers 9, 10 and 8, introducing monthly budget monitoring and examining the spending practices of cost centers that performed within budget.

Proposed budget reductions should also be tested against different spending scenarios before approval.

## Limitations
